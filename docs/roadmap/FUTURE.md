# Future Vision

Once the MVP is established, LifeOS will expand into a comprehensive platform.

## Proposed Features

### 1. Voice Interface
Interact with LifeOS hands-free using voice commands processed by LLMs, ideal for when you are walking to the bus stop.

### 2. IoT Integration
Connect with smart home devices (e.g., turning off lights when leaving for class, flashing a light when it's time to catch the bus).

### 3. Predictive Analytics
Analyze historical transit times to predict delays before they are officially announced.

### 4. Advanced Health & Habit Tracking
Correlate sleep data or fitness data with daily productivity and schedule adherence.
