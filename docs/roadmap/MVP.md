# Minimum Viable Product (MVP) for LifeOS

The immediate next step is to transition the current static app into a dynamic MVP.

## Scope
1. **Dynamic Schedule Management:** Move away from hardcoded `data/schedules.ts` to a local database or CMS.
2. **Calendar Integration:** Allow users to import their university schedule via iCal/Google Calendar.
3. **UI Polish:** Refine the interface to act as a "Dashboard" rather than just a bus schedule.

## Out of Scope for MVP
- Complex AI recommendations.
- Full event-driven architecture.
- Multi-user support.
