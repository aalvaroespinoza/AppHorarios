# n8n Automation

n8n serves as the nervous system for LifeOS background tasks.

## Why n8n?
It allows for visual workflow building and easily connects to hundreds of APIs without writing boilerplate integration code.

## Planned Workflows
1. **Weather Sync:** Fetch weather data every morning and push it to the LifeOS database.
2. **Calendar Sync:** Daily sync of university schedules.
3. **Transit Alerts:** Scrape or poll transit authority websites for strikes or delays, triggering a webhook to LifeOS.

## Deployment
n8n will be self-hosted alongside the LifeOS backend to ensure data privacy.
